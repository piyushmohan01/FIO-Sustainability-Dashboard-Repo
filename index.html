FIO Food Environmental Impact Seasonal Explorer (FIO-EISE)

This dashboard shows the environmental impact of food and drink purchases across a calendar year (2022) from a major UK supermarket retailer. Sales from loyalty card holders living in Yorkshire and the Humber region were linked to product-level environmental sustainability estimates to show how the carbon, land use, and water use footprints of all purchases change over time and across different food categories.

About the Metrics

The dashboard allows picking from four different environmental metrics:

  • GHGE = Greenhouse Gas Emissions (kg CO2-equivalent)
  • LU = Land Use (agricultural land area [m2]s multiplied by length of time occupied [years])
  • WU = Water Use (agricultural fresh water withdrawals in litres)
  • A 'Composite Impact' score, calculated by scaling each of the above metrics between 0 and 1,
    and calculating the normalized average of the three scaled metrics.
    The Composite Impact score gives an indication of overall environmental impact, weighting GHGE, LU and WU equally.

Environmental impact data were derived from Poore and Nemechek’s global averages for food and drink commodities [1], and were linked to products as described here [2]. The system boundaries are from cradle to store gate.

How can this dashboard be used?

The dashboard has two charts, which allow both a high-level overview of environmental footprint trends across a full year, as well as exploration of environmental footprints down to individual food categories.

  1. TIMELINE CHART: Shows one smoothed trend line across the year per metric, rising when purchasing was more environmentally intensive. The line shows the 7-day smoothed environmental footprint across the selected period. A peak means purchasing was more intensive on those days. The chart can be configured as follows:
    • Choose Metric: Select a metric (e.g. GHGE, LU etc.) using the dropdown at the top of the chart.
    • Choose Unit: Switch between Total SF and Per kg. Total SF tells us the total environmental impact from product sales, which depends on the types of products purchased, and the volumes in which they are purchased. Per kg footprints tells us about the mix or composition of products purchased, and whether this mix is more or less sustainable. This metric controls for the total volume of products purchased, which will vary across the year (e.g. increasing at Christmas).
    • Choose Date Range: Narrow the date range using ‘From’ and ‘To’ date pickers to zoom into any period. The y-axis rescales automatically. For the Composite metric the y-axis always starts from zero so Total SF and Per kg remain directly comparable.
    • Explore Values: Hover anywhere on the chart to snap to the nearest data point. The tooltip shows the smoothed value, the full-year average, and how far above or below that average the selected date was.
    • Event Overlay: Use the drop-down list to select different events to overlay on the graph (e.g. public holidays, school holidays, or key weather events).


  2. HEATMAP CHART: Shows how the environmental impact of individual food categories varies across the year, revealing category-level seasonal trends. Each row is one food category. Each column is one week. The colour of a cell shows how that category's average daily footprint over the week compared to its own typical level for the year. Darker indicates the footprint is above average for that row. The scale is per row (i.e. category) so colour cannot be used to compare magnitude across rows (i.e. categories). The chart can be configured as follows:
    • Choose Metric & Unit: Select a metric (e.g. GHGE, LU etc.) using the dropdown at the top of the chart. Use the Total SF or Per kg toggle to change what the colours represent. The row (i.e. category) order updates to reflect the most impactful categories for the active metric and unit combination. In Per kg view the colour scheme changes to a diverging scale where the midpoint is the category's annual mean.
    • Choose Number of Categories: Set how many categories to show using the top N dropdown. The default is 30 categories.
    • Explore Data:
      • Rank: Annual rankings for the selected environmental impact metric can be found to the right of the heatmap. The rankings tell you how the categories annual footprint compares to all other categories (1 = highest, 30 = lowest environmental impact).
      • Annual mean SF: This metric is found in the top right of the chart. It shows the mean daily sales footprint for the chosen environmental impact metric (e.g. GHGE) across all products sold.
      • Annual mean per kg: As above, but per kg, rather than total sales footprint.
    • Hover on any cell to read the week’s footprint statistics::
      • ‘Week’s Rank’: this tells you how the week’s footprint ranks to all other categories for that week (1 = highest, 30 = lowest environmental impact)
      • 'Deviation from the annual mean': this tells you how the week’s footprint differs from the average footprint for that category across the year. For example, a deviation of +10% for the category ‘chocolate’ means that the daily footprint for chocolate was 10% higher that week, compared to the average daily footprint for chocolate across the year.
      • 'Annual contribution to total SF': this statistic only shows when the ‘Total SF’ metric is selected. It gives the percentage contribution for a given category to the total sales footprint across all products. For example, a value of 10% for the category ‘pasta products’ would tells us that across the year, sales of products in this category contribute to 10% of the total annual sales footprint across all products.

Dashboard Controls

  • Metric (Dropdown): Switch between Carbon (GHGE), Land Use (LU), Water Use (WU), and Composite (Timeline Chart only)
  • Total Sales Footprint / Per-kilogram (Toggle): Volume derived Sales Footprint vs. basket composition per-kg for each metric (with composite)
  • Date Range (Date pickers): Select start and end dates for period individually (zoom into window)
  • Reset Date (Button): Reset date period to go back to default (full date range in data, entire calendar year)
  • N categories (Dropdown): Number of categories to show (Top-N categories ranked for environmental impact. Options include choosing to show 10, 30, or 65 categories at a time, Heatmap Chart only)

Suggested Steps to Get Started:

  1. Select a metric from the dropdown above to update both charts (a reminder of the metric definitions can be found on the left-hand side).
  2. Toggle between Total SF and Per kg to switch between Sales Footprint and Per-kg composition.
  3. Use the 'From' and 'To' date pickers to narrow both charts to a period of interest.
  4. Hover on any data point, markers or cell to read exact values.
  5. Use the drop-down to select events of interest (e.g. public holidays).
  6. Reset dates returns both charts to the full year view (no change to other inputs).
