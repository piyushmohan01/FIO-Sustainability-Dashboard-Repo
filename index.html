This dashboard shows the environmental impact of food and drink purchases across a calendar year (2023, dummy) from a major UK supermarket retailer.

Sales from loyalty card holders living in Yorkshire and the Humber region were linked to product-level environmental sustainability estimates to show how the carbon, land, and water footprints of all purchases change over time and across different food categories.

Dashboard allows picking from 3 different environmental metrics to focus on:
A. GHGE = Greenhouse Gas Emissions
(Carbon impact in kg of CO₂-equivalent)
B. LU = Land Use
(Land area required in sq. metres per year)
C. WU = Water Use
(Fresh Water consumption in litres)
Composite score is a normalized average of all 3 metrics, scaled between 0 and 1.

Two charts allowing full-year overview down to individual food category behaviour.

TIMELINE CHART: Starting point for analysis that shows one smoothed trend line across the year per metric, rising when purchasing was more environmentally intensive.

HEATMAP CHART: Shows the impact divided by food category, revealing which categories were responsible for changes in trend.

> Select a metric from the dropdown above to update both charts (view help at top-right).
> Toggle between Total SF and Per kg to switch between Sales Footprint and Per-kg composition.
> Use the From and To date pickers to narrow both charts to a period of interest.
> Hover on any data point, markers or cell to read exact values.
> Reset dates returns both charts to the full year view without changing any other setting.

Reading the timeline

The line shows the 7-day smoothed environmental footprint across the selected period. A peak means purchasing was more intensive on those days.

Select a metric using the dropdown at the top of the chart. Switch Total SF and Per kg to compare volume-driven changes against composition-driven ones. If both views peak in the same period, food volumes and basket intensity both increased together.

Narrow the date range using From and To to zoom into any period. The y-axis rescales automatically. For the Composite metric the y-axis always starts from zero so Total SF and Per kg remain directly comparable.

Hover anywhere on the chart to snap to the nearest data point. The tooltip shows the smoothed value, the full-year average, and how far above or below that average the selected date was.

Reading the heatmap

Each row is one LCFS food category. Each column is one week or day. The colour of a cell shows how that category's footprint compared to its own typical level for the year. Darker means above average for that row. The scale is per row so colour cannot be used to compare magnitude across rows.

Use the metric dropdown and Total SF or Per kg toggle to change what the colours represent. The row order updates to reflect the most impactful categories for the active metric. In Per kg view the colour scheme changes to a diverging scale where the midpoint is the category's annual mean.

Set how many categories to show using the top N dropdown. Start with 30 for analysis. Switch between Weekly and Daily columns using the view toggle. Weekly view is better for spotting seasonal patterns. Daily view shows event timing and day-of-week structure.

Hover on any cell to read the footprint value, the category's rank, its share of the full year's total, and how far above or below its annual average the period was. The period total for all 65 categories is shown in sidebar below the global metric updates when the date range changes.
