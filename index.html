FIO Food Environmental Impact Seasonal Explorer

This dashboard visualises the environmental impact of food and drink purchases at a major UK grocery retailer across a full calendar year. Sales transactions are linked to per-product environmental estimates to show how the carbon, land, and water footprint of food shopping changes over time and across food categories. It is a prototype built on synthetic dummy data. Real 2022 retailer data will be connected following validation in the Trusted Research Environment.

How to use the timeline chart

The timeline shows a smoothed weekly trend for the selected metric across the year. Select a metric from the dropdown and toggle between Total SF and Per kg. Use the date pickers to zoom into any period. For Composite Impact the y-axis always starts from zero. Hover anywhere on the chart to snap to the nearest data point and read the value, annual mean, and deviation.

How to use the category heatmap

Each row is one food category, each column is one week. Cell colour shows how that category compared to its own typical level for the year and not to other categories. Use the top N dropdown to set how many categories are shown. Hover a cell to read the weekly mean, rank, annual share, and deviation from annual mean. Hover an event marker to see the event name and date.

Reading the hover values

Value or Weekly mean: actual footprint for this category in this period with units.
Week's rank: position among all 65 categories. Rank 1 is the highest impact category.
Deviation from annual mean: how unusual this period was. A result of +40% means 40% above the category's typical level.
Annual contribution: fraction of the full year's total footprint. Shown for Total SF view only.
